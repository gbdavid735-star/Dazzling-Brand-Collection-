About Dazzling Brand Collection

Who We Are

Dazzling Brand Collection (DBC) is a clothing brand based in Enugu, Nigeria. We sell quality and affordable wears for men - from shirts, t-shirts, trousers, shorts, boxers, pams, to shoes, belts and traditional wears.

We believe everyone deserves to look dazzling without spending too much. That's why we bring you different brands, cultures and sizes - all in one place.

What We Sell

  • Shoes & Pams - Sneakers, corporate shoes, pams
  • Trousers & Shorts - Jeans, chinos, short nika
  • Shirts - T-shirts, corporate shirts, double-hand shirts
  • Accessories - Belts, boxers, watches, hats
  • Traditional - Native wears for all occasions
